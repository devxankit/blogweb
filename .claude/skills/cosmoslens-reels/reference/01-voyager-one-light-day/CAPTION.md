## English version (cosmoslens-voyager-one-light-day-en.mp4)

Send Voyager 1 a message today… and it arrives tomorrow. 🛰️

On 18 November 2026, Voyager 1 becomes the first human-made object one full light-day from Earth: about 25.9 billion km away. Launched in 1977, it still talks to us on roughly 22 watts.

On board is a golden record carrying an Indian raga: Kesarbai Kerkar's "Jaat Kahan Ho" (Raag Bhairavi). Where are you going?

We checked NASA JPL's orbit data: after 18 November, Voyager 1 never comes back within one light-day of Earth.
